## Faithfulness and changes

Line-by-line port of geometry, polygonArea, ringArea, rad and isClosed from index.js at the pinned commit, including the implicit closing of unclosed rings and the same summation order. The ring operation exposes upstream ring() with signed output. Inputs are structure-checked (positions [lon, lat] within range) instead of silently returning undefined for unknown types.

## Reference values

Illinois and whole-world geometries and the point, linestring, rotated-ring, unclosed-ring and degenerate-ring cases are from upstream test/; their literal expectations are asserted to 12 significant digits as upstream does. All full outputs were generated by executing pinned upstream index.js in Node, not this port; supplementary cases are labelled.

Frozen references are in tests/references.json; each case has an `origin` that distinguishes literal upstream fixtures from values generated by executing pinned upstream code. No full-upstream-suite claim is made.
