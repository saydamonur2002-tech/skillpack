# Source and changes: Fixed-timestep game loop scheduler (MainLoop.js animate)

Upstream: https://github.com/IceCreamYou/MainLoop.js at commit `3ddadf0ab902da96af7077be40206ac27ba1b1d3` (1.0.4), license MIT.

## What was taken

animate() fixed-timestep accumulator, fps moving average, panic guard, setMaxAllowedFPS and resetFrameDelta

## Port notes

Line-by-line port of animate(): minFrameDelay skip, frameDelta accumulation, fps exponential moving average every >1000 ms, update loop with the 240-step panic break, draw(frameDelta/timestep). start() is modelled as the first timestamp drawing once and resetting lastFrameTimeMs/lastFpsUpdate. discard_on_panic models the documented end() pattern calling MainLoop.resetFrameDelta().
