## Scope and failure

Date is TT, not UTC or UT1; input bounds are arithmetic limits, not an assertion of model validity over millions of years. Precession and frame bias only: no nutation, aberration, Earth rotation, polar motion, ephemeris or complete apparent-place calculation. Matrix direction is GCRS -> mean-of-date for column vectors; transpose reverses this orthogonal transform. Scientific use requires a documented epoch/model validity assessment.
