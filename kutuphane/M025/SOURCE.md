## Faithfulness and changes

Port of encode, decode_bbox, decode, neighbors, ensure_valid_lon and ensure_valid_lat from main.js at the pinned commit: longitude-first bit interleaving, strict greater-than bisection, lower-case decode, neighbour offset of two half-errors and JavaScript % semantics (math.fmod) for longitude wrap. Invalid characters are rejected by schema instead of producing NaN.
