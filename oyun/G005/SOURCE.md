# Source and changes: A* path finding on a tile grid (python-pathfinding)

Upstream: https://github.com/brean/python-pathfinding at commit `e271d46f53b39081b5ee33036f875c42f07d5f52` (main@2026-10-27), license MIT.

## What was taken

AStarFinder, Finder, Grid, GridNode, SimpleHeap, heuristics and backtrace (vendored byte-for-byte under tool/pathfinding)

## Port notes

The upstream modules are vendored unmodified (byte-identical to upstream/) under tool/pathfinding; the Skillpack wrapper adds character-to-cost mapping, bounds and blocked-cell checks, grid.cleanup() between queries, cost reporting (end node g) and a time limit of 8 s instead of the upstream 10 s so the pack watchdog never fires first.
