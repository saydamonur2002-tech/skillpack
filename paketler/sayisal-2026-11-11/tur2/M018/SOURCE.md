## Faithfulness and changes

Ports eraCal2jd integer arithmetic, including truncation-toward-zero for (month-14)/12. The rest of the dividends are nonnegative in the admitted domain. Unlike ERFA status -3, this adapter rejects an invalid day rather than returning a computed JD alongside an error status. Caps the otherwise broader upper-year range at 9999. Full ERFA BSD license and source SOFA-heritage notice are retained; this is an ERFA-derived adaptation, not an official SOFA distribution.

## Reference values

Original t_cal2jd literally asserts date 2003-06-01 -> djm0=2400000.5 and djm=52791.0. Additional negative/zero/century/leap-year reference values were generated by compiling the exact pinned ERFA C routine. The valid-date results have exact integer/half-integer expectations.

Frozen references are in tests/references.json. Origins distinguish literal upstream fixtures, native-generated differential values, cross-library analytic formulas and supplementary mathematical properties. No unsupported digit guarantee or full-upstream-suite claim is made.
