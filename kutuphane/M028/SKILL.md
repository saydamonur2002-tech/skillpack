# Destination point, bearing, midpoint and cross-track distance on a sphere

Spherical-earth navigation helpers: the point reached from a start after a distance on a bearing, initial and final bearing between two points (with a 16-point compass label in English and Turkish), great-circle midpoint, haversine distance and cross-track distance from a path. Answers "where am I after 500 m to the north-east?" or "which direction is the ferry pier?".

## Scope and failure

Spherical earth model (default mean radius 6371 km): errors up to about 0.3% versus the WGS-84 ellipsoid; use M003 (Vincenty) for ellipsoidal precision. Straight great-circle geometry, not a road route. Bearings are degrees clockwise from true north (not magnetic). Coincident points give null bearings. Distance limited to half the equator per call.

## Faithfulness

Ports distanceTo, initialBearingTo, finalBearingTo, midpointTo, destinationPoint, crossTrackDistanceTo and equals from latlon-spherical.js plus Dms.wrap90/wrap180/wrap360 from dms.js at the pinned commit, with the same operation order (degrees*PI/180) and JavaScript remainder semantics (math.fmod). NaN bearings are returned as null. The compass label is a Skillpack addition (16 sectors of 22.5 degrees). Text parsing of degree-minute-second strings is not exposed.
