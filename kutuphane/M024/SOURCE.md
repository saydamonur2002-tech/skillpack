## Faithfulness and changes

Port of py2_round, encode, polyline.encode and polyline.decode from src/polyline.js at the pinned commit: Python-2-style rounding, delta from previous rounded point, zigzag sign mapping, 5-bit little-endian chunks with 0x20 continuation and offset 63. JavaScript float shifting (coordinate /= 32, & 0x1f, multiply-by-shift accumulation) is replaced by equivalent exact integer arithmetic within the documented 2^52 bound. fromGeoJSON/toGeoJSON are not exposed.
