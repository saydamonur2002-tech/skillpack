# Source and changes: 2D collision detection and push-out vector (SAT.js: circles, boxes, convex polygons)

Upstream: https://github.com/jriecken/sat-js at commit `20e612681d1f9eabc9ea34dc98c4d27f985ffec6` (0.9.0), license MIT.

## What was taken

testCircleCircle, testPolygonCircle, testCirclePolygon, testPolygonPolygon, isSeparatingAxis, voronoiRegion, Polygon._recalc and Box.toPolygon

## Port notes

Port of SAT.js test functions with Vector math inlined. Response.clear() defaults (aInB=bInA=true, overlap=Number.MAX_VALUE) kept. Box is converted by Box.toPolygon. Polygon setPoints duplicate-point removal and setAngle rotation kept. Skillpack additions: input validation rejects clockwise, degenerate or concave polygons (SAT.js silently gives wrong answers there) and push_a = -overlapV.
