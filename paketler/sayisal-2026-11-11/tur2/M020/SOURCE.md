## Faithfulness and changes

Ports eraPmat06 -> eraPfw06/eraObl06 -> eraFw2m, preserving all polynomial coefficients, TT-century calculation and Rz(gamb), Rx(phib), Rz(-psib), Rx(-epsa) source rotation order/sign conventions. Identity construction and row rotation semantics mirror eraIr/eraRx/eraRz. Reports angles as additional transparent intermediates. Does not merely return a generic rotation matrix; the reusable technique is the IAU bias-precession model. Full ERFA and SOFA-derivation notices are retained.

## Reference values

All nine literal t_pmat06 matrix entries are preserved for (2400000.5,50123.9999); off-diagonal checks retain the upstream 1e-14 scale. Additional matrices were generated by compiling the full exact pinned original C pipeline. Orthogonality and positive determinant are supplementary properties, not replacements for those references.

Frozen references are in tests/references.json. Origins distinguish literal upstream fixtures, native-generated differential values, cross-library analytic formulas and supplementary mathematical properties. No unsupported digit guarantee or full-upstream-suite claim is made.
